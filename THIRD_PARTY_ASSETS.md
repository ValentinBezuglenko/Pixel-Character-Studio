# Third-party character examples

The editor includes three editable 16×16 character-animation examples:

| Example | Author | Source | Terms stated by the source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sara | nightgane | [OGA Sara 16×16](https://opengameart.org/content/oga-sara-16x16-1bit-color) | CC0 |
| Miss Princess | qubodup | [Miss Princess Animated 16×16](https://opengameart.org/content/miss-princess-animated-16x16) | CC0 |
| Green Traveler | Meafabulis | [Free Animated Character Asset 16×16](https://meafabulis.itch.io/pixel-art-sprites-free-animated-character-asset) | The source permits editing and personal or commercial use; credit is appreciated |

The examples are stored directly in `index.html` as editable pixel data. Review the linked source pages before redistributing the examples separately from this project.
